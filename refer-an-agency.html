Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Who qualifies as a referred agency?

Any UK-based letting agency, property management company, or estate agency with a lettings portfolio that is not currently an active client of Complete Digital Vetting.

What does the referred agency receive?

The referred agency receives full, dedicated onboarding support from our UK team and access to our 30-day free referencing trial so they can test our speed, platform, and reporting risk-free.

When do we receive our free month of referencing?

When the agency you referred completes their onboarding, completes their trial period, and becomes a qualifying active customer, we apply a full month of free referencing to your account.

Can we refer more than one letting agency?

Yes! There is no limit to the number of agencies you can recommend. For each qualifying new agency that becomes an active customer, you earn an additional month of free referencing.
